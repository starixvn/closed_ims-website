Calculation Details
pls details how to calculate in this area.
a) for March
the launch date is from 12-march-13 so the data from 1-11 Mar is testing data, we cant bill client for this period.
when we do the billing, we will take the data from 12-31 Mar.
we agreed to waive them 1 mth. So from 12-31 Mar, we cant bill them the min fee.
in this month, we will bill them the actual usage 8000.
* as long as under waiver period, we will charge the actual usage.
for April
the 1-Apr --> 12 Apr is under waiver period.
So we will take the actual usage fee from 1-Apr to 12 Apr = 9000
from 13 Apr ---> 30 Apr, min fee applied (15000/30*18) = 9000
the actual usage fee is 8000.
So we will take the greater of actual usage fee or the min fee.
In this case, we have to take min fee 9000.
Total april bill = 9000+9000=18000
b) for March
we count from the launch date.
12-Mar to 31-Mar is under waiver period. So we will take the actual usage fee = 12000
for April
1 Apr to 12-Apr is under waiver period. So we will take the actual usage fee = 1500
from 13 Apr ---> 30 Apr, min fee applied (15000/30*18) = 9000
We will take the greater of actual usage fee or the min fee.
in this case, still the min fee greater than the actual usage 2000.
So we will take the prorata min fee = 9000
total april bill = 1500+9000=10500
